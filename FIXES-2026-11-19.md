# Decision Ledger — booked-material returns were not credited (Ring 6x6)

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Branch:** `arena/00a0d161-amsv6` · **Status:** fixed, tested

## The symptom

The Decision Ledger *Overall Material Summary* showed **Ring 6x6** with a
negative remaining:

| | Booked | Delivered | Remaining |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| qty | 74.7 | 87.1 | **−12.4** |
| value | 19,795.50 | 23,081.50 | **−3,286.00** |

Every other row of the table satisfied `remaining = booked − delivered ≥ 0`.

## Root cause

Reconstructed against the `AMS_FULL_20261116-044312.amsdb` snapshot:

* 15 Nov — booking **SB-BK-1007** (Malik Amir Sahb Tibba): 100 × Ring 6x6 @ 265.
* 15 Nov — delivery **SB-SL-1009**: 87.1 dispatched, fully allocated (old remaining 12.9).
* Between snapshots — a **Booked Return of 12.4** was posted and the *booking
  cancel* flow wrote off the restored pool: cancellable
  `= booked − (OUT − returns) = 100 − (87.1 − 12.4) = 25.3`, so the line's
  `qty` became `100 − 25.3 = 74.7`. Physically that balances: the client holds
  `87.1 − 12.4 = 74.7` against a booking of 74.7.

Three modules already credit Booked Returns — the cancel plan
(`booking_cancel_plan.py`), the sale-allocation pool
(`_allocate_booking_quantities_for_sale_item`) and the material ledger page.
The **decision ledger counted `type='OUT'` rows only**, so it kept reporting
87.1 delivered against 74.7 booked and printed −12.4 / −3,286.00
(= 12.4 × 265 exactly).

## The fix

`app/blueprints/ledgers/other.py` (route `decision_ledger`):

* collect Booked Return rows per client with the **same tagging as
  `booking_cancel_plan`** (`type='IN'`, `nimbus_no='Material Return'`,
  `transaction_category`/`client_category = 'Booked Return'`), keyed by
  `booked_material or material`;
* delivered = `max(0, dispatches − returns)` — the clamp mirrors the cancel
  plan, where an over-return leaves the full booked obligation standing;
* everything downstream (per-client badges, overall summary, totals,
  `_material_values` pricing) consumes the net figure, so the value columns
  and the unpriced-units warning follow automatically.

`templates/decision_ledger.html`: summary footnote now states that delivered
is net of booked-material returns.
